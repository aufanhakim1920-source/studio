Features

Three things it does properly.

Gets the loading right

Evenings, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays each carry their own multiplier. Set your base rate once and it applies the rest.

Checks your payslip

Type in what you were actually paid. If it doesn’t match the shifts you logged, you get told which day is short and by how much.

Keeps the record

Every shift stays logged with times and rates, exportable as a PDF — the thing you want if you ever have to raise it with payroll.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is this legal advice?

No. Penalty does arithmetic on the numbers you give it. If something looks wrong, it points at the day — what you do next is between you and your employer, or the Fair Work Ombudsman.

Do I need to know my award?

No. You enter your base hourly rate and pick your industry; the loadings come from there. You can override any multiplier if your agreement differs.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Shifts are logged on the device and sync later, which matters in a basement kitchen.

Can I cancel?

Any time, and you keep every shift you logged. Export is free on both plans.
