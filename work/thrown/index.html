The firing 6

    Thrown, not moulded

    Every piece is turned on a wheel, so no two are quite the same size. That is the point of it.

    Fired twice

    Bisque, then glaze at 1240°C. Vitrified, so it holds heat and does not stain.

    Dishwasher fine

    All three glazes are food-safe and machine washable. Not for the microwave if it has a gold rim — ours do not.
