about

I'm Muhammad Raufan Hakim Rachmad — Aufan. First-year Data Science student at the University of Melbourne, originally from Surabaya, Indonesia. I've been running my own life since 15, and I build things constantly: storefronts, games, tools, motion graphics.

Most of what I make starts as a problem I actually have. A store that needed to look like a real brand. A schedule nobody could read. A game I wanted to play. I ship them end to end — design, frontend, backend, deploy — and I learn the stack on the way if I don't have it yet.

Away from the screen I'm front-of-house at a Carlton North bakery, I compete in Taekwondo, and I've led hackathon teams to two consecutive first-place finishes as a first-year against masters-level competition.
