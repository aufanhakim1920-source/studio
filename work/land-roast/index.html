Bean temperature against time. Where you stop is the entire argument. First crack is the bean's own water flashing to steam and blowing it open; second crack is the cell walls giving way.

03 — Why it tastes like that

Every acid the farm grew is still in there.

Heat does two opposite things at once: it destroys the fruit acids the farm grew, and it builds browned sugar out of what is left. They cross a little after first crack — which is the whole difference between sour-fruity and chocolate, then smoke.

04 — The four we sell

Land the bean in a band and hold it — the bag names itself. The higher it grew, the lighter we take it: dense beans hold their acids, and burning them is burning what you paid for.
