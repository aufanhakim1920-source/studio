    01 — the ink library

    Six drums.
    We run them one at a time.

    Riso ink is soy-based and it sits on the paper rather than soaking in, so a second pass does not cover the first — it multiplies with it. That is the whole trick, and it is why we sell passes.

    02 — paper

    Uncoated, always.
    Coated stock rejects the ink.

    Pick a stock above and the sheet changes under the ink — every overprint on this page is computed against the paper you chose, because that is what actually happens on the drum.
