MOTION NIGHT

Fifteen short films,
made to learn one thing at a time.

Every one is a Remotion build — React that renders to video, frame by frame. They are exercises, not products: each was made to answer one question, measured against real reference films, and several were deleted the same night for failing. Filter and sort them below rather than scrolling — the numbers are the point.

What these are evidence of. One: a measurement is not a verdict — the film with the most real craft here scored worst on every gate, and one that scored highest contained no animation at all. Two: choosing the right subject beats fixing the film afterwards. Three: a written rule changes nothing; only a tool that refuses to let the work continue ever changed anything.

Films 11–26 and 31 are not here. They were made, judged and deleted; the numbers are retired and never reused.
