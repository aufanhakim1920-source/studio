05 — ONE HONEST LIMITATION

A forecast is not a lifeguard. Fathom models tide, swell, wind and water temperature. It does not see rips forming, it does not know the sandbank moved last week, and it has never been in the water. Sixty-one mornings last year it said don't — and it will have been wrong on some of them, in both directions.

Fathom is a concept — spec work exploring forecast design, not a real product
