Selected work

  1. Booking system · live

    Week Board

    A booking page that reads a real Google Calendar live and shows the next two weeks of free time. Someone picks an open slot, it writes a provisional hold straight onto the calendar, and I approve or decline from an email — the visitor never makes an account. Same shape as a booking page for a café, a studio or a clinic.

    Self-initiated — built for my own schedule, and running now.

    Google CalendarEmail approvalNo login

  2. Accounts & database · live

    Biomate

    People sign in, get their own private data, upload photos and message a group. Eight tables in Postgres, every one with row-level security, so what you can read and write is decided by the database rather than by the page. Points and streaks are calculated in SQL instead of stored, so they can’t be faked from the browser.

    A university team project for a hackathon — not client work.

    Sign-inPostgresRow-level security

  3. Catalogue site

    baka nae.

    My sister sells her own illustrated merch on Shopee, where she looks like any other seller. I built her a site that runs off a single catalogue file — filter by series or product type, sort by price, page through, in Indonesian or English. Adding a new product category takes a line of data, not a line of code.

    I built the site. The shop is hers — 10,711 followers and 4.97★ from 7,440 ratings on Shopee are her numbers, not mine. Unpaid, for family.

    Filters & sortTwo languagesData-driven
