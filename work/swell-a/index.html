The honest part
A forecast is not a lifeguard

Fathom models tide, swell, wind and water temperature. It does not see rips forming, it does not know the sandbank moved last week, and it has never been in the water.

61 mornings last year it said "don't" — and it will have been wrong on some of them, in both directions.
